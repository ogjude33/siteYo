Booking terms

Good to know.

How do I secure my booking?

A R100 non-refundable deposit is required to secure all bookings. The balance is payable when the service is complete.

How do you price larger houses?

Home cleaning prices depend on house size. For homes with 3 or more bedrooms, we provide a quote after a free viewing or after seeing photos.

What do I need to provide?

Please make sure we have access to water and electricity. We bring our cleaning products unless we agree otherwise with you.

What happens if I cancel?

If you cancel less than 12 hours before your booking, you forfeit your deposit.
